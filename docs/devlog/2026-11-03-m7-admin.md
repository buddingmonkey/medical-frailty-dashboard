## Step 1: backup download and restore (13:35–14:00 builder; lead merge to 14:25)
- `GET /api/admin/backup` streams a **zip** (`fflate` 0.8.3) with one JSON file, format 2.
  It holds every state-data table, read 500 rows at a time. It leaves out the reference,
  derived and FTS tables, and says so in a `notes` field. A test fails if a new table is in
  neither list. R2 keys stay out, because `docs/security.md` says they never leave the Worker.
- Seed (51 states): 13,735 rows, a 5.4 MB JSON zipped to 237 kB in ~0.3 s, 44 queries.
- `/admin/restore` takes the zip or a per-publish `.json` (format 1). The admin picks one
  state and version, and it comes back as a **draft** through the existing draft API, then
  checks and publish. A missing source PDF is kept as a citation ("file not available").
  Mappings come back only when ticked, after a diff.
- Zip-bomb guard: one top-level `.json` only, and inflation stops past 50 MB. Without the cap,
  `fflate`'s `unzipSync` would have inflated a bomb in full; inflating in 16 KB slices bounds
  memory.

## Step 2: weekly cron backup and cleanup (14:15–14:36 builder; lead review to 14:50)
- A Cron Trigger (`0 7 * * SUN`, both environments) runs `runMaintenance`. Each part has its
  own try/catch, and the result goes in `app_meta.last_maintenance`. The parts:
  - the weekly zip dump (with R2 keys, for restore path 3), keeping 12;
  - per-publish backups kept to 20 per state;
  - open drafts idle for 30 days deleted; discarded drafts emptied after 30 days, with
    their rows kept.
- `/admin/restore` gained a Maintenance panel: the last run, the weekly dumps with downloads,
  and "Run maintenance now".
- Migration 0015 adds `dataset_versions.updated_at`, backfilled from the audit log.
- The zip is buffered in memory (capped at 32 MB; real data is ~1 MB) and written with one
  put. Multipart would need 5 MB parts.

### Corrections and surprises
- **The weekly dump needs Workers Paid.** A Cron Trigger gets 10 ms of CPU on Free; the
  dump's JSON and deflate take 65–160 ms. On Paid, a weekly trigger gets 15 min. The
  project lead stays on Paid. `docs/deployment.md` says what to do on a downgrade.
- The builder did not watch its tests fail first. The lead broke the code four ways: allow
  published versions, keep 21 backups, expire at 28 days, keep 13 dumps. Each break failed
  a test.
