## Goal
Finish the admin tools: backup download and restore, a weekly backup with cleanup, and a
remap screen that shows what changes before saving.

## What happened
1. 13:34 CST: the project lead said "start M7". Wave 1 ran steps 1 (backup and import) and
   3 (remap) in parallel; step 2 (weekly cron) waits for step 1's backup writer.
2. The project lead asked that the backup download be a zip. The builder was told while it
   was working and switched to a streamed zip (`fflate`).
3. **Step 3, remap** (13:35–14:10, builder; 2 review rounds):
   - On `/admin/categories`, checkboxes and "Select all" remap many categories or chapters
     at once.
   - Preview shows, per change, how many published entries move and how many stay (because
     a comparison-category placement or a chapter override wins). It shows a sample, and
     the state's counts before and after.
   - Save re-checks a token and answers 409 if anything changed.
   - The cost is 4 reads per preview and one write per changed mapping, plus 3. Federal
     categories are worked out when a page is read, so a remap recomputes nothing.

## Decisions
| Decision | Why | Who decided |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The backup download is a zip | Smaller, and opens anywhere | Project lead |
| A change that moves published entries always goes through the preview, a single row's too; "Confirm" stays instant only when it moves no published entry (the API checks) | One code path; nothing moves unseen | Lead (main session) |
